# Code of Conduct

[Project overview](README.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Purpose and scope

The DataTable-AltEditor community welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Everyone should be able to contribute in an environment free from harassment and discrimination.

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project communication channels. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and use professional language.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect differing perspectives, experience levels, and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing another person's personal information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct harmful behavior, and respect requests to stop.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or encouragement of violence.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual attention, or inappropriate sexual content.
- Publishing private information without permission or threatening to do so.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its resolution.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting concerns

To request a private reporting channel, open an [issue](https://github.com/bensitu/DataTable-AltEditor/issues) asking to contact a maintainer about a community concern. Do not include names of affected people, allegations, or other sensitive details in the public request. Share the report only after a maintainer provides a private channel.

In the private report, describe what happened, where and when it occurred, any relevant links or evidence, and how you would prefer to be contacted. Share only information necessary to understand the concern. You do not need to confront the person involved before reporting.

Maintainers should limit access to reports to those who need the information to address the concern and avoid publishing identifying details without consent. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action requires sharing relevant information. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome; request another maintainer where one is available.

## Response and enforcement

Maintainers assess reports fairly, consider available context, and choose a response proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a request to correct behavior, removal of inappropriate content, a warning, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions.

When appropriate, maintainers explain the decision and any conditions for continued participation privately to the people concerned. Anyone affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the same private channel and provide additional context. Response times depend on maintainer availability.
