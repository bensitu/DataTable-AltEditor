Cell editing

Double-click a cell to edit it. Enter saves, Escape cancels, and Tab or Shift+Tab saves and moves within the row. Leaving a control cancels its unsaved value.

Names are required, email addresses must be valid, and ages must be between 0 and 120. Sorting or paging cancels unsaved edits. Saves take a short time to demonstrate pending requests.
