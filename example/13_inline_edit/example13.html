DataTable-AltEditor / Example 13

Cell editing

Double-click a cell to edit it. Enter saves, Escape cancels, and Tab or Shift+Tab saves and moves within the row. Leaving a control cancels its unsaved value.

Names are required, email addresses must be valid, and ages must be between 0 and 120. Sorting or paging cancels unsaved edits. Saves take a short time to demonstrate pending requests.

Rendered dropdowns and inline editing

Change Status directly with its dropdown. Double-click Task or Note to edit; Tab skips Status. The dropdown change handler updates the table data locally, independently of AltEditor save callbacks and the failed-save checkbox above. Reloading resets this table.
